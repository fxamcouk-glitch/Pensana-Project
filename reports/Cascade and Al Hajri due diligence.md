# Cascade's Qatar backing rests on self-description

Cascade Natural Resources Limited, Pensana's US$165m "Qatar-backed" strategic investor, is **Guernsey company no. 77418**. It was **incorporated on 27 January 2026**, seven weeks after Pensana announced an unnamed investor, and its registered office is care of a licensed trust company, Artemis Trustees, at Admiral Park, St Peter Port ([GLEIF record](https://api.gleif.org/api/v1/lei-records/2549009RGS5TCPWQ7Z89)). Its owners are not public. Its LEI record reports no consolidating parent, and no Qatari state body has said it stands behind the company. The claim of "State of Qatar" backing rests only on statements by Cascade and Pensana ([RNS 6 Aug 2026](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/pensana-plc-cascade-financing-060000879.html)). Its chairman, Lloyd Pengilly, is a real and verifiable former JPMorgan mining banker. In 2015 his previous Qatar-backed vehicle, QKR, stopped making new investments after its Qatari backer reviewed its mining allocation ([BizNews/Bloomberg](https://www.biznews.com/briefs/2015/07/30/former-jse-star-lloyd-pengillys-qkr-scraps-expansion-plans-on-mining-rout)). **Only US$15m of the US$165m has been paid.** No public evidence shows that Cascade holds or can obtain the remaining **US$150m**, so Pensana cannot yet rely on it. UK Companies House records support this picture. They show the US$15m allotment of 13,992,537 shares at 80p. They show no Cascade nominee on Pensana's board, no charge in Cascade's favour and no UK company for Cascade. Dr Badr bin Dalhim Al-Faheid Al-Hajri, the Cascade board nominee, is **Qatari, not Saudi**, on the evidence of Arabic-language press. He is a real Doha-based figure who leads an Arab League-affiliated AI association. Nothing found substantiates the "Sheikh" or "Dr" titles, any QIA or state role, or any mining track record. None of this shows wrongdoing by anyone. What it shows is that the deal's credibility depends on parties whose capacity cannot be verified from public records.

## A nine-month-old Guernsey vehicle with no visible owners

Every registry fact about Cascade comes from the global LEI system, GLEIF. GLEIF rates this record **"fully corroborated"** against the Guernsey Registry. The record gives Cascade as a company limited by shares, registered as 77418 and active. It was incorporated on 27 January 2026 and obtained its LEI on 8 May 2026, five days before the threshold date on its first holding notification ([GLEIF record](https://api.gleif.org/api/v1/lei-records/2549009RGS5TCPWQ7Z89)). GLEIF lists **no direct or ultimate parent**. Both fields carry the exception "NON_CONSOLIDATING", meaning that no parent includes Cascade in its group accounts ([GLEIF ultimate-parent exception](https://api.gleif.org/api/v1/lei-records/2549009RGS5TCPWQ7Z89/ultimate-parent-reporting-exception)). That fits ownership by individuals, trusts or a group of investors. It fits less well with a subsidiary of a state fund that consolidates its holdings, although it proves neither. Only one entity named "Cascade Natural Resources" exists in GLEIF worldwide. There is none at UK Companies House and none in SEC EDGAR. This means the "Cascade Natural Resources (US)" label in Pensana's 8 June 2026 RNS has no registered US entity behind it ([CH search](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/search/companies?q=Cascade+Natural+Resources); [RNS 8 Jun 2026](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/pensana-plc-longonjo-mine-development-060000799.html)).

The registered agent, **Artemis Trustees Limited**, is a licensed Guernsey fiduciary. In June 2022 the Guernsey Financial Services Commission fined it **£450,000** for "serious and systemic" financial-crime control failings. These included failures to establish clients' source of wealth and to understand who owned and controlled client structures ([GFSC public statement](https://www.gfsc.gg/news/artemis-trustees-limited-0)). That record is about the service provider, not about Cascade. It matters only because counterparties may be relying on the provider's checks of Cascade's beneficial owners. The Guernsey Registry website was unreachable during this research (expired certificate and HTTP 503) and was not bypassed. Cascade's registered directors, shareholders and annual validations are therefore **unconfirmed**. Guernsey's beneficial-ownership register is not public in any case.

Cascade's public presence is thin. The domain cascadenaturalresources.com was **registered on 15 December 2025**, six days after Pensana's unnamed-investor RNS, through Hostinger and for one year ([RDAP](https://rdap.org/domain/cascadenaturalresources.com)). The site appears to have been made with the Lovable AI site builder. It names no team, backers, portfolio or address. Its only news item is the 18 June release announcing Al-Hajri's appointment, which on 19 October still says he "Joins Board" ([site bundle](https://cascadenaturalresources.com/assets/index-D4r96MoE.js)). The order of events is: deal announced, then domain registered, then company formed, then investor named, then LEI obtained. On that sequence, Cascade appears to have been formed for the Pensana transaction rather than being an established fund with a track record. This is an inference, not a registry finding.

## Pengilly's record shows a QKR precedent, not a 2019 Auryn sale

Companies House holds a single officer record for **Lloyd Crossland Pengilly**, British, born May 1954. It shows one appointment, as director from 3 September 2012 of QKR Corporation Management (UK) Ltd, a company **dissolved in January 2017**. He has **no current UK directorships** and no role in the Pensana group ([CH appointments](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/officers/VZTVOE03Rg4NO0ee_LHGPxjm-z0/appointments)). His co-directors there were Andre Liebenberg and Roger Kennedy ([CH officers](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/08136349/officers)). The one surviving UK QKR company, QKR Corporation Investments (UK) Ltd, is not his. Since May 2024 its person with significant control has been **Sebastian Kulczyk**, and its directors are corporate-services nominees ([CH PSC](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/08913264/persons-with-significant-control)). That suggests the QKR platform passed to the Kulczyk family's side. Pensana describes Pengilly as a former chairman of JPMorgan Africa and of its Metals & Mining division ([RNS 17 Apr 2026](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/pensana-plc-us-165-million-060000917.html)), and Bloomberg profiles him as president of QKR Corp ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/person/4745848)). Africa Confidential names him in a 2012 "Guinea loan saga" who's-who and a 2018 piece about former JPMorgan bankers. Both articles are paywalled and were not read, so **no allegation can be drawn from them** ([Africa Confidential](https://www.africa-confidential.com/profile/id/3710/Lloyd_Pengilly)).

The QKR history is the most relevant precedent. Pengilly and Jan Kulczyk founded QKR in 2012. According to Reuters, its largest backers were **Qatar Holding, part of QIA**, and Kulczyk Investments, which "provided almost half of QKR's funds" each ([Miningmx/Reuters](https://www.miningmx.com/off-the-wires/13150-qatari-second-thoughts-negative-for-qkr/)). QKR bought AngloGold Ashanti's Navachab gold mine in Namibia for an enterprise value of US$110m, completing on 30 June 2014 ([AngloGold 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067428/000120561314000025/aga_announcement.htm)). By July 2015 it had disbanded its acquisition team, after management changes at the sovereign fund and Kulczyk's death had cost it backing for a roughly US$1bn approach to Nevsun ([BizNews/Bloomberg](https://www.biznews.com/briefs/2015/07/30/former-jse-star-lloyd-pengillys-qkr-scraps-expansion-plans-on-mining-rout)). This research found **no evidence of a 2019 sale of Navachab to Auryn Mining**, so that premise is not supported. In October 2021, QKR Namibia, which held 92.5% of Navachab, was exploring a sale through UBS ([The Namibian](https://www.namibian.com.na/qkr-explores-exit-from-navachab)). In June 2024 it was still the owner when RMB arranged US$155m of expansion debt. That 2024 report does not mention Pengilly ([Namibian Sun](https://namibiansun.com/mw-main/qkr-secures-n2.9-billion-for-navachab-expansion2024-06-06118681)). The parallel with Cascade is an analogy, not evidence: a Pengilly vehicle relying on Qatari capital completed one deal and then stalled when the Qatari side reconsidered.

## The US$150m cannot be relied on from public evidence

Cascade's only known deployed capital is the **US$15m paid in April 2026**, for 13,992,537 shares at 80p. That holding cost about £11.2m and is worth about **£5.6m at the current ~40p**. The remaining US$150m, for 38.2% of the Portuguese holding company Sable Min, has been described as "due shortly" since at least August. The 6 August RNS also said that Cascade was in "continued discussions at the highest levels" with "its backers, the State of Qatar" ([RNS 6 Aug 2026](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/pensana-plc-cascade-financing-060000879.html)). On 25 September Pensana confirmed it had received US$15m of the US$165m. It said it had ramped down work at Longonjo and was in "detailed financing discussions" with its major shareholders ([RNS 25 Sep 2026](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/pensana-plc-longonjo-financing-coola-060000806.html)). There has been no further RNS, payment notice, TR-1 or termination since then. The 16 October AGM notice does not mention Cascade, Qatar or Pengilly, and proposes re-election of the existing five directors only ([AGM notice](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/2026-10-16-PENSANA-AGM-NOM.pdf)).

The stakes are larger than the cash itself. The interim results say the Cascade money is meant to satisfy "the equity funding conditions precedent for the Absa debt financing facility" ([H1-26](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Pensana-Interim-results-for-the-six-months-ended-31-December-2025.pdf)). The US$160m senior debt is therefore effectively waiting on Cascade too. US$150m for 38.2% of Sable implies a holding-company value of about US$393m. Pensana's entire market capitalisation is about £141m, so the market is pricing a low chance of completion. Several facts are known, none of which is proof on its own: the vehicle was formed after the deal was announced, no parent reports it, no Qatari body has confirmed backing, payment has repeatedly been called imminent, and the QKR precedent stalled. Together they mean the **capacity to pay cannot be established**. Payment appears to depend on a Qatari funding decision that is not public. Whether the US$150m is legally committed, and on what long-stop date and termination rights, is **unknown**, because the long-form agreement has not been published. Sanctions screening of the OFAC SDN and consolidated lists and the UK OFSI list, carried out on 19 October, found **no matches** for Cascade, Pengilly, Al-Hajri, QKR or Artemis ([OFAC SDN](https://www.treasury.gov/ofac/downloads/sdn.csv); [UK OFSI ConList](https://ofsistorage.blob.core.windows.net/publishlive/2022format/ConList.csv)). EU and UN lists and court records were not searched.

## Companies House shows the cash in, but no charges, nominees or Cascade footprint

Pensana Plc (no. 12206525) made 16 filings between January 2025 and 19 October 2026: eight share allotment returns (SH01) and two replacements, one charge registered and later released, two confirmation statements with no updates, one set of accounts and one set of AGM resolutions ([CH filing history](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12206525/filing-history)). The issued share count rose from 290.3m to **353,515,226**. The latest allotment was Cascade's 13,992,537 shares at £0.80 on 27 April 2026, and nothing has been allotted since. The allotments reconcile exactly to the RNSs. They include the FSDEA debt conversion of 23,148,148 shares at **24p**, made in the same window as an 80p cash placing ([RP01 replacement SH01](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12206525/filing-history/MzUwMjE1NDI5NWFkaXF6a2N4/document?format=pdf&download=0)). That points to loose company-secretarial control, but the matters are minor and CH has issued no notices. The confirmation statements of September 2025 and September 2026 contain no shareholder list, which is normal for a traded company. Shareholder identities therefore come only from TR-1s ([CS01 2026](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12206525/filing-history/MzU0NzQwOTU1OGFkaXF6a2N4/document?format=pdf&download=0)).

There have been **no officer appointments or terminations since September 2024**. ## Al-Hajri is Qatari, a real public figure, but his titles are unsubstantiated

**He is Qatari, not Saudi.** The belief that he is Saudi most likely comes from the Al-Hajri tribal name, which is widespread in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province, Kuwait and Bahrain as well as Qatar. No source found ties this individual to Saudi Arabia. Two Arabic outlets state his nationality outright. Al-Araby Al-Jadeed's headline reads "The Qatari Badr Al-Hajri chosen as president of the Arab Union for Artificial Intelligence" and gives his full name, بدر بن دلهم الفهيد الهاجري ([Al-Araby Al-Jadeed](https://www.alaraby.co.uk/entertainment_media/%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D8%AF%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%B1%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%B3%D8%A7%D9%8B-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A8%D9%8A-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A)). Al-Tareeq described him in December 2025 as a "Qatari citizen" marking Qatar National Day at his home in Bani Hajer, Al Rayyan, and added his grandfather's name, Ali ([Al-Tareeq](https://www.altreeq.com/524561)). ## Conclusion

The research separates two things that Pensana's announcements present together. The people are real: Pengilly is a verifiable mining banker, and Al-Hajri is a verifiable Qatari public figure. The money and the state backing are not verified. The "Qatar-backed" label currently rests on a Qatari national with an AI-association role, a chairman whose last Qatari-funded vehicle stalled, and a Guernsey shell administered by a trust company, rather than on any statement from QIA or the Qatari state. For Pensana holders, the practical conclusion is to treat the US$150m as a contingent option, not as funding. A financing plan led by FSDEA and M&G, which Pensana disclosed in September, is the base case until Cascade produces proof of funds or a Qatari body confirms its backing.

Concrete next checks:

1. **Guernsey Registry extract for company 77418.** Obtain the directors, registered shareholders, annual validations and any filing showing Al-Hajri's appointment. Check whether Pengilly is a registered director and whether Artemis personnel sit on the board.
2. **FY2026 annual report, due the week of 19 October 2026.** Read the going-concern note and any material uncertainty, cash at 30 June 2026, post-balance-sheet events on Cascade (payment, long-stop date, termination), the Sable and Ozango cap table, the RiverFort repayment and any Saltend impairment.
3. **Tyranna Resources' ASX filing on the Coola farm-in.** Reconcile Tyranna's two-stage, roughly US$100k-cash terms with Pensana's "US$2.6m over three years" description, as a test of how reliable Pensana's partner disclosures are.
