# Pensana's US$165m Qatar lifeline stalls at US$15m

Pensana Plc (LSE: PRE) is a pre-revenue rare earths developer whose one material asset, the Longonjo mine in Angola, is **over 30% built but under-funded and deliberately slowed**. Of the US$165m that Guernsey-registered, "Qatar-backed" Cascade Natural Resources committed in March 2026, **only US$15m has arrived**. The project's US$160m senior debt (Absa, with a US EXIM guarantee) is still unsigned. On 25 September 2026 the company ramped down design, high-cost site work and long-lead procurement and pushed first production to "late 2028" ([RNS 25 Sep 2026](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/pensana-plc-longonjo-financing-coola-060000806.html)). The shares fell 24% that day and trade near **40p**, against a **181p** closing peak in October 2025 and Cascade's 80p entry price ([Yahoo price data](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/PRE.L?range=2y&interval=1d)). The partners who have actually paid in are few. Angola's sovereign wealth fund FSDEA owns about 28–29% of the plc, put US$25m into the mine company and is a secured lender. M&G holds about 9.6%, and Cascade has paid its US$15m. Every offtake is a non-binding MOU, the EXIM support is a non-binding Letter of Interest, and the Qatari state backing has never been confirmed by any Qatari body. The audited accounts have carried a **going-concern material uncertainty** in each of the last two years. The FY2026 annual report (year to 30 June 2026) is due **the week of 19 October 2026** and had not been published when this report was prepared. Review it as soon as it appears, because it will be the first audited view of cash after the Cascade stall. **The private Telegram investors group was not analysed.** Access needs the user's own export, so that section is pending.

## Public channels show a US pivot, a quiet management and a loud bear

Pensana runs two websites. pensana.co.uk is the corporate and investor-relations site, and ozango.co.ao belongs to the Angolan operating subsidiary Ozango Minerais S.A., which describes itself as holder of the Longonjo exploitation rights and publishes its ESIA. The corporate homepage now leads with "establishing a U.S. mine-to-magnet supply chain from the world's largest rare earth mine… with U.S. partnerships and backing from U.S. Gov." It still carries legacy copy saying "Pensana's Saltend project will be unique to the UK" ([pensana.co.uk](https://pensana.co.uk/)). CEO Tim George's board bio still describes the Humber Freeport processing hub, which was scrapped in October 2025 ([Board page](https://pensana.co.uk/board-of-directors/)). The board is unchanged: Paul Atherley (Chairman, also chair of Alkemy Capital), Tim George (CEO), Rob Kaplan (Finance Director) and independent NEDs Steve Sharpe and Alison Saxby. No Cascade or Qatari nominee sits on it.

LinkedIn is thin from the outside. The company pages are behind a login wall. Indexed profiles exist for Atherley, Kaplan and Ozango site staff ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/paulatherley/)). One undated snippet resharing Angolan press said Longonjo "will start only in 2028, one year later than initially planned" while Pensana was still saying 2027, which was an early public sign of the slip ([LinkedIn snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/benedito-dumbo-a97334107/)). No senior departures or job postings were found. The last senior appointment found was Karen Brown (COO) taking charge of Longonjo in September 2025. On X, @PensanaRE was promoting Atherley's Sky News appearance on US supply chains and "@NASDAQ listing plans" in October 2025 ([X](https://x.com/PensanaRE/status/1978711610643378597)). No 2026 podcast or video interview with Atherley or George was found. Since the Cascade money stalled, management has spoken mainly through RNS. The exception was George's attendance at a private White House briefing on US$3bn of critical-minerals support on 10–11 August 2026. Shares fell 5.3% that morning and no US funding for Pensana was announced ([Mining Weekly](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/pensana-attends-us-presidential-briefing-on-rare-earths-supply-2026-08-11)).

The main forums (LSE.co.uk, ADVFN, Reddit) blocked automated access, so no forum posts were read directly. The loudest public critic is Tom Winnifrith at ShareProphets. He has run a "fake or not fake sheikh" campaign and claims Longonjo is "effectively shuttered" ([ShareProphets 25 Sep](https://shareprophets.com/views/88441/vindication-pensana-the-150m-cheque-from-qatar-is-still-not-in-the-post-any-update-on-the-fake-or-not-fake-sheikh); [ShareProphets 28 Sep](https://shareprophets.com/views/88481/pensana-two-questions-paul-atherley-must-answer)). These are opinion pieces and partly paywalled. "Shuttered" overstates the company's disclosed position, which is "ramped down", not shut. Kalkine headlines in October 2026 asked whether Pensana had "just banked fresh funding" linked to Qatar. No RNS between 25 September and 16 October confirms any new money ([Kalkine](https://kalkine.co.uk/news/mining/pensana-shares-slide-rare-earth-ambitions-meet-another-bout-of-market-scepticism)). No formal short-seller report, FCA action, litigation or environmental incident was found.

Angolan press is supportive and state-aligned, but it is candid about the delay. During the mining minister's September 2026 site visit, Ozango's chairman Alcídio José put the slip down to investment mobilisation ("A mobilização de investimentos criou algum atraso"). Ozango reported 25% completion, newly identified dysprosium and terbium, about 150 farmers compensated, 98 staff and a planned commissioning in October 2027 with operations in 2028 ([Ver Angola](https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/092026/MateriasPrimas/50045/Longonjo-volta-a-surpreender-descoberta-de-novas-terras-raras-eleva-potencial-da-mina.htm)). UK regional coverage is the opposite in tone. The Hull/Saltend refinery, which was expected to create about 126 jobs, was dropped in favour of the US. Atherley said UK support had amounted to only about £5m, and the Department for Business and Trade called the decision "disappointing" ([Argus](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2743508-uk-re-hopes-dashed-as-saltend-refinery-scrapped)). No public Pensana Telegram channel was found. The private investors' group is outside this review, as noted above.

## RNS record: big numbers announced first, smaller amounts received, then corrections

Read in sequence, Pensana's announcements follow a recurring pattern: a large headline figure, a smaller realised amount, then a dated clarification. In March 2025 the company reported "full financing totalling circa US$268 million" from Absa, the Africa Finance Corporation and FSDEA, all at subsidiary level and subject to documentation ([RNS 18 Mar 2025](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/update-on-longonjo-finance/8783753)). Only FSDEA's equity was ever disclosed as received. AFC's US$81.2m debt share and US$54.9m convertible loan do not appear in any 2026 RNS, and debt is now described only as the "proposed US$160 million ABSA debt funding package (U.S. EXIM Guaranteed)" ([RNS 4 Mar 2026](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/increased-us-165-million-strategic-investment-/9457728)). No announcement says AFC has withdrawn. Its absence is an inference from silence, but a strong one.

The strategic investment changed several times between December 2025 and April 2026. On 9 December 2025 an unnamed investor was to subscribe US$100m for 95m new shares, subject to due diligence ([RNS 9 Dec 2025](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/us-100-million-strategic-equity-investment/9283991)). In February 2026 the figure became US$160m and a general meeting was called. On 4 March 2026 it became US$165m from Cascade, restructured as **US$15m for 3.8% of Pensana at 80p** plus **US$150m for 38.2% of Sable Min**, the Portuguese holding company that owns the group's stake in Ozango. The general meeting was adjourned sine die, and completion was expected "in the coming weeks" ([RNS 4 Mar 2026](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/increased-us-165-million-strategic-investment-/9457728)). The 95m shares were never issued. The US$15m arrived on 17 April 2026 ([RNS 17 Apr 2026](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/us-165-million-strategic-investment-to-suppor-/9525090)). By 6 August the second tranche was "due shortly", and Atherley called the delays "frustrating" ([RNS 6 Aug 2026](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/pensana-plc-cascade-financing-060000879.html)). On 25 September Pensana turned to "detailed financing discussions" with its major shareholders.

The first-production date has moved repeatedly. It was "late 2026" in April 2025, then early 2027, then 2027 "on target, on budget" as late as 8 June 2026. A 10 August RNS about US policy said 2028 in passing, and on 25 September it became "late 2028". That is roughly a two-year slip in 18 months ([RNS 2 Apr 2025](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/epcm-and-updated-ore-reserve-estimate/8809512); [RNS 8 Jun 2026](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/pensana-plc-longonjo-mine-development-060000799.html); [RNS 10 Aug 2026](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/trump-announces-us-3-billion-support-for-u-s-/9712159)). The 25 September RNS calls 2028 the "previously announced" date, although every dedicated schedule update before August said 2027. Capex moved over the same period from a US$217m envelope described as "intact" in July 2025 to a "US$250 million development" in June 2026, with no reconciliation. The Nasdaq listing slipped from "by Q1 2026" to "mid-2026" to "preparations underway", and the share consolidation needed for it was dropped from the 2025 AGM ([RNS 30 Sep 2025](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/date-of-annual-general-meeting-and-proposed-s-/9139174); [RNS 10 Nov 2025](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/notice-of-annual-general-meeting-and-form-of-/9222467)). The Saltend cancellation reached the market through BBC and Times reports on 16–17 October 2025. No matching RNS was found in that window, and the shares fell about 40% over nine sessions from the 181p peak ([Investegate list](https://www.investegate.co.uk/company/PRE)). No trading halts or suspensions appear in the record.

## Financial statements show a cash-thin company dependent on FSDEA

The latest audited figures (30 June 2025) show a company that was close to insolvent on a current basis: **cash of US$0.81m against current liabilities of US$31.4m**. Cash had fallen to **US$40,133 at 31 December 2024** ([AR25](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Pensana-integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf); [H1-26](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Pensana-Interim-results-for-the-six-months-ended-31-December-2025.pdf)). By 31 December 2025 cash had recovered to US$19.6m. That came almost entirely from FSDEA's one-off US$25m subscription into Ozango and about US$11.8m of plc placings. Of that cash, US$10.2m was ring-fenced for Longonjo, and only US$7.0m was available for corporate costs. Borrowings of US$11.3m were all current: the FSDEA bridge (US$8.66m, secured on Pensana's Ozango shares) and a 12% external facility (US$2.6m) secured by charges over Sable Min, SBLRTHS and Saltend Magnet Metals. Monthly cash burn, operating plus capex, rose from about US$0.7m in FY25 to about US$3.7m in H1 FY26 as main construction began, and capital commitments jumped to **US$22.3m** ([H1-26](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Pensana-Interim-results-for-the-six-months-ended-31-December-2025.pdf)).

Auditor BDO LLP (lead partner Jill MacRae) gave an unmodified FY2025 opinion with a **"Material uncertainty related to going concern"** section. Going concern was a key audit matter in both FY2024 and FY2025. The directors' going-concern basis depended on the Longonjo financing meeting its conditions precedent, FSDEA converting its loan, new money to pay **overdue UK contractors**, and those contractors continuing to support the company. BDO obtained legal confirmations that no proceedings had been started over "historical unsettled creditor balances" ([AR25](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Pensana-integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf)). The interims repeat the material uncertainty and say that, if the main financing fails, the group "would need to refinance or restructure the FSDEA facility" ([H1-26](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Pensana-Interim-results-for-the-six-months-ended-31-December-2025.pdf)). Both assumptions behind that basis have since weakened: the US$268m package has been replaced, and Cascade's US$150m has stalled. A material uncertainty in the FY2026 accounts is therefore very likely, and it may be sharper than before. This is an expectation, not a fact, until the report is published.

The filings show other points to watch. Saltend's planning permission lapsed after FY25 and renewal was deferred. Even so, management found "no impairment indicator" for about **US$17.8m** of Saltend intangibles and assets under construction at December 2025, which makes a write-down in FY2026 a live question. Directors have repeatedly funded the company personally. Kaplan sold his own shares to lend to the company and was owed **£420,642** at December 2025. Executive salaries were accrued unpaid, while FY25 pay totalled about US$2.5m, mostly share-based awards priced at 23–24p ([AR25](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Pensana-integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf)). Wood Plc's historic invoices were settled in shares at £1.20 ([RNS 9 Oct 2025](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/issue-of-equity/9161362)). The interims also contain internal inconsistencies. The net-debt reconciliation's cash flows do not match the cash flow statement, and the related-party note says the FSDEA conversion was into Ozango equity, whereas the equity note and the RNS say plc shares were issued ([H1-26](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Pensana-Interim-results-for-the-six-months-ended-31-December-2025.pdf)). Issued shares rose 24%, from 285.2m (July 2023) to **353,515,226** (April 2026). The larger dilution of Longonjo itself is happening at subsidiary level, where the plc share count does not show it. Pensana's Ozango stake fell from 84% to 68.4% after FSDEA's subscription. If Cascade completes, Cascade's 38.2% of Sable would cut Pensana's look-through interest to roughly 42%. That figure is calculated from disclosed percentages, and the company says the Ozango split will be re-cut.

Cascade is the main question. Its TR-1 gives a registered office at Admiral Park, Guernsey ([TR-1 22 Jul 2026](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/holding-s-in-company/9682568)), and its website showed only the title "Global Energy & Mining Investment" ([cascadenaturalresources.com](https://cascadenaturalresources.com/)). Its chairman, Lloyd Pengilly, is a former JPMorgan Africa and Metals & Mining chairman. He has run a Qatar-backed mining vehicle before: QKR Corp, which was backed by Qatar's sovereign wealth fund, made one acquisition (Navachab gold, US$110m) and then stopped new investments in 2015 after Qatari support for a larger deal fell away ([BizNews/Bloomberg](https://www.biznews.com/briefs/2015/07/30/former-jse-star-lloyd-pengillys-qkr-scraps-expansion-plans-on-mining-rout)). Pensana's disclosures say only that the backers are "specialist family offices and major sovereign funders" and "the State of Qatar". No QIA, ministry or other Qatari statement confirms this, and Rare Earth Exchanges noted that the release "does not name the family offices or sovereign funders" ([Rare Earth Exchanges](https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/pensana-secures-first-15m-tranche-but-mine-to-magnet-ambitions-still-face-execution-risk/)).

The board-appointment episode adds to the doubts. On 18 June 2026 an RNS titled "Strategic Investor Board Appointment" said Sheikh Dr Badr bin Dalhim Al-Faheid Al-Hajri "has joined" Cascade's board. It was not an appointment to Pensana's board, despite the title. The shares fell 11% ([RNS 18 Jun 2026](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/strategic-investor-board-appointment/9624404)). Two months later Pensana said the appointment was "not yet effective" and had not been filed with the Guernsey Registry, and it redirected enquiries to Pengilly ([RNS 17 Aug 2026](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/update-on-strategic-investor-board-appointmen-/9724082)). Cascade's TR-1, submitted on 3 June, reached Pensana only on 22 July. Neither event is evidence of wrongdoing. Both point to loose coordination and to inside information being published before it was true. Pengilly and one other nominee are to join Pensana's board only "on completion", so the governance rights that would come with US$150m are not yet in place either. Valuation is a further consideration. US$150m for 38.2% of Sable implies about **US$393m post-money** for the holding company, while Pensana's whole market capitalisation is about £141m. The market is pricing a low probability of completion.

The other partners carry smaller but real risks. FSDEA has the most influence. It is the largest plc shareholder, a 31.6% Ozango shareholder by implication from the company's 68.4%, and a lender secured on Pensana's Ozango shares. If Cascade fails, FSDEA's decisions will largely determine whether Longonjo is completed and on what terms for plc shareholders. Ozango's own statement gives a different split (Pensana 67.6%, FSDEA 26.7%, minorities 5.7%), a discrepancy the annual report should resolve ([Ver Angola](https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/092026/MateriasPrimas/50045/Longonjo-volta-a-surpreender-descoberta-de-novas-terras-raras-eleva-potencial-da-mina.htm)). The Coola farm-out conserves cash but brings in little: about US$100k in cash, according to Tyranna's account, which describes a two-stage earn-in to up to 70% (50% for US$100k plus US$1.5m of spend in two years). Pensana describes it as US$2.6m over three years diluting it to 20% ([Mining.com.au](https://mining.com.au/tyranna-secures-angola-rare-earths-earn-in-deal/)). The descriptions of distance also vary: 30km, 40km and "60km northwest of Huambo". On the offtake side, Toyota Tsusho, ReElement and VAC/eVAC are credible names, but every agreement is non-binding with pricing unagreed. The US DFC's support quoted in the VAC announcement went to eVAC, not Pensana. Pensana's own DFC link is a US$3.4m technical-assistance grant, of which about US$0.5m had been drawn by June 2025 ([AR25](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Pensana-integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf)). No National Wealth Fund, Afreximbank, Standard Bank, Sonangol, Endiama or IDC involvement was found.

## Conclusion

The investment case now rests on one unresolved question: will the State of Qatar, through a Guernsey vehicle with no visible staff or portfolio, pay US$150m into a Portuguese holding company? If it does not, the realistic paths are a rescue led by FSDEA and M&G at a heavily discounted price, or more dilution at project level, with plc holders left owning a shrinking share of Longonjo. The asset has not failed. The resource is large, construction is 30% complete and Angolan state support is solid. What has eroded is the credibility of Pensana's financing disclosures, and the share price already reflects that more than the asset risk.

Three things will test this view in the coming weeks. The first is the FY2026 annual report, due the week of 19 October 2026: check its cash at 30 June 2026, the going-concern wording, any Saltend impairment, the Ozango and Sable cap table, and any disclosure of Cascade's long-stop date or conditions. The second is any RNS confirming or abandoning Cascade's second tranche before the 13 November AGM. The third is primary confirmation from counterparties that this research could not obtain: a Guernsey Registry extract for Cascade, Tyranna's ASX announcement, and EXIM's or AFC's own position. The private Telegram investors group is the remaining open item and needs the user's own export before it can be analysed.
