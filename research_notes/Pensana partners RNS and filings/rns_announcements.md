## 1. Longonjo (Angola): development progress, construction, first production and financing

### Takeaway
Longonjo went from "fully funded" (March 2025) and main construction (May 2025) to a project 30%+ built but **under-funded and slowed**. Only US$15m of Cascade's US$165m has arrived, the ABSA/EXIM debt is still not closed, capex has risen from US$217m to US$250m, and first production has slipped repeatedly: late 2026, then 2027, then 2028, then "late 2028".

### Cited Findings
**Context (2023–2024)**
- 3 Apr 2023: Pensana needed US$550m in total for Saltend and Longonjo (a US$175m green bond via ABG Sundal Collier, a US$220m strategic equity investor and a US$150m debt package), with first production targeted for 2025. — [INV 3 Apr 2023](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/update-on-financing-and-project-development/7424887)
- 27 Jun 2023: revised staged plan with upfront capex of US$200m. FSDEA (the Angolan sovereign wealth fund) provided an immediate US$15m loan as part of a broader US$80m investment, and ABSA was mandated for a US$120m debt facility. — [INV 27 Jun 2023](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/update-on-longonjo-financing-and-development/7598463)
- 31 Oct 2023: modular redesign at US$200m capex. Funding was to be at the Ozango level (84% Pensana), with Pensana's stake expected to fall to ~64% at a "read-through valuation of 66 pence per share". — [INV 31 Oct 2023](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/update-on-finance-for-the-longonjo-and-salten-/7849672)
- 26 Feb 2024: non-binding term sheet for up to US$156m of project debt (maximum 7-year tenor, conditional on export credit agency guarantees). Capex US$217m including a 10% contingency. — [INV 26 Feb 2024](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/longonjo-project-debt-finance/8054994)

**2025**
- 23 Jan 2025: Absa conditionally approved a credit term sheet for its 50% participation in a ~US$160m syndicated loan to 84%-owned Ozango Minerais. Debt would be ~60% of project funding and equity at the Ozango level ~40%.   - It has negotiated with potential major offtake partners and intends to sign offtake agreements "when required to support the debt facilities with ABSA and EXIM".
  - "Outstanding detailed construction design activities, high-cost site activities and long term procurement items have been ramped down until the financing has been finalised."
  - The schedule is "expected to be extended from the previously announced 2028 to late 2028." — [Yahoo mirror 25 Sep 2026](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/pensana-plc-longonjo-financing-coola-060000806.html); [INV 25 Sep 2026](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/update-on-longonjo-financing-and-coola-farm-out-/9790537)

### Inferences
- **First-production guidance history:** late 2026 (Apr 2025), then early 2027 with talk of accelerating to late 2026 (Oct 2025), then 2027 "on target" (Jan–Jun 2026), then 2028 (10 Aug 2026, stated in passing in a US-policy RNS, not a dedicated schedule update), then late 2028 (25 Sep 2026). That is roughly a two-year slip in 18 months.
- **Capex history:** US$200m (2023), then US$216.5–217m (2024–Jul 2025), then US$250m (Jun 2026). The Jun 2026 figure may include the HREE circuit or other scope; the RNS does not reconcile it.
- **Who is paying today:** the "fully funded" US$268m package of March 2025 appears to have dissolved apart from FSDEA's equity. AFC's US$81.2m debt share and US$54.9m convertible are **not mentioned** in any RNS after 2025. Debt is now described as "ABSA debt funding (U.S. EXIM Guaranteed)" and is still "proposed" (4 Mar and 17 Apr 2026). This is an inference from silence; AFC's withdrawal has not been confirmed.
- **Cash and project risk:** with Cascade's US$150m stalled and long-lead procurement and design "ramped down", there are near-term cash and execution risks. The ~US$135m of commitments (Jun 2026) may carry cancellation or storage costs.

### Gaps
- No RNS gives Longonjo's cash on hand, spend to date after June 2026, or a revised capex after the slowdown.
- The status of AFC's debt and convertible commitments is not addressed in 2026 RNSs.
- The FY2026 annual report (due the week of 19 Oct 2026) was not yet available and may include a going-concern statement.

## 2. Saltend (UK), downstream and US plans, and the Coola farm-out

### Takeaway
The UK Saltend refinery was dropped as a UK-sited project; the company reported a US switch in October 2025 media coverage rather than in a dedicated RNS. Its FEED work is being re-engineered into modular separation trains to be sited at the "most advantageous location", alongside a US "mine-to-magnet" strategy (eVAC, ReElement, EXIM, Nasdaq). Coola was farmed out on 25 Sep 2026 to ASX-listed Tyranna Resources: US$2.6m of spend over 3 years earns Tyranna enough to dilute Pensana to 20%.

## 3. Financing RNSs: Cascade, the "$15m tranche", Qatar backing, placings, convertibles and loans

### Takeaway
Corporate funding has come from small placings (35p in 2025, 80p in late 2025–2026), loan-to-equity conversions and contractor share settlements. The headline strategic deal grew from an unnamed "US$100m" investor (Dec 2025) to "US$160m" (Feb 2026) and then a restructured Cascade "US$165m" (Mar 2026), mostly at subsidiary level. Only the US$15m PLC subscription at 80p has been received (April 2026). The US$150m into Sable remained outstanding as of 25 Sep 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Placings, issues and loans (2024–2026)**
- 28 Mar / 23 Jul 2024: two directors provided a £2m loan facility.
