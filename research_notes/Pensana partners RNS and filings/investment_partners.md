# Pensana Plc (LSE: PRE): Investment, Financing, Strategic and Commercial Partners

Research date: 19 October 2026. Primary sources are Pensana RNS notices, read in full from investegate.co.uk, and Pensana's March 2026 interim results PDF. Secondary sources are mining and financial press. Where a statement comes only from Pensana, that is stated. "UNVERIFIED" marks items that no independent or counterparty source confirms.

Group structure, for reference: Pensana Plc (UK) owns Sable Min Unipessoal Lda (Portugal). Sable is the majority shareholder of Ozango Minerais S.A. (Angola), which develops the Longonjo mine. SBLRTHS Unipessoal Lda holds the Coola exploration interest (see Coola section), and Saltend Magnet Metals Limited is the UK subsidiary for Saltend. Pensana's shareholding in Ozango fell from 84% to 68.4% after FSDEA's US$25m subsidiary-level equity — [RNS 4 Feb 2026](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/update-on-us-25-million-longonjo-mine-finance/9407060). The external loan facility is secured by a charge over Pensana's shares in Sable Min, SBLRTHS and Saltend Magnet Metals — [Interim results 27 Mar 2026, note 12](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Pensana-Interim-results-for-the-six-months-ended-31-December-2025.pdf).

## Q1. Who or what is "Cascade" (the US$165m Cascade financing; "$15m tranche", April 2026)? Ownership, principals, track record, terms

### Takeaway
Cascade Natural Resources Limited is a Guernsey-incorporated investment company with a registered office at Admiral Park, Guernsey. It is chaired by Lloyd Pengilly, a former JP Morgan banker, and Pensana describes it as "Qatar-backed". It committed US$165m: US$15m for 3.8% of Pensana, which has been paid, and US$150m for 38.2% of Sable Min, the holding company for Longonjo, which is still outstanding about six months later. Cascade's beneficial owners and its Qatari backers have not been publicly identified. Pengilly's previous Qatar-backed mining fund, QKR Corp, made one acquisition and then stopped new investments in 2015.

### Inferences
- The unnamed "Strategic Investor" of Dec 2025 (US$100m, 95m shares) appears to be Cascade. The 4 Mar 2026 RNS presents the Cascade deal as following on from the 9 Dec 2025 and 10 Feb 2026 announcements and as an increase to them. The deal changed from 95m Pensana shares (about 28% of the company) to US$15m at plc level plus US$150m for 38.2% of the project holding company. The headline amount went US$100m, then US$160m, then US$165m.
- Valuation implied by the terms: US$150m for 38.2% of Sable values Sable at about US$393m post-money. Sable holds 68.4% of Ozango (before any re-adjustment).
- Red flags, as observations rather than allegations: (a) the US$150m has been outstanding for about six months, with repeated statements that it is due "shortly"; (b) the scope has widened during the delay; (c) the backers are unnamed beyond "the State of Qatar"; (d) no confirmation of the deal could be found from QIA or any Qatari government body; (e) the Qatari director's appointment was announced before it took effect (see Q3); (f) the "(US)" label conflicts with the Guernsey domicile; (g) Pengilly's earlier Qatar-backed vehicle stalled when sovereign support was withdrawn.

## Q4. Who took the Coola farm-out (25 Sep 2026 RNS), and on what terms?

Pensana describes US$2.6m of spending over three years, after which Pensana's interest would fall to 20%. Tyranna describes a two-stage earn-in to up to 70%: the first stage is 50% for US$100k cash plus US$1.5m spend within two years. Tyranna signed through its subsidiary Luvulu (Mauritius) on 28 Sep 2026 with SBLRTHS Unipessoal (Pensana's subsidiary), Coola Mining and Coola's local shareholders. Tyranna is a micro-cap whose shares trade at A$0.002.

## Q5. Angolan partners and government links: FSDEA, lenders for Longonjo (Absa, AFC, Afreximbank, Standard Bank, BFA, Sonangol, Endiama, IDC)

### Takeaway
FSDEA, Angola's sovereign wealth fund, is Pensana's largest shareholder and main project funder, holding about 29% of Pensana through its subsidiary ASF Yova Mining Holding Limited. FSDEA also lent a US$15m bridging loan, of which about US$8.7m was outstanding at Dec 2025, and put US$25m of equity into Ozango, giving it about 31.6% of Ozango. Senior debt was first a US$160m Absa (50%) plus AFC (US$81.2m) syndicate approved in March 2025. By Oct 2025 it had been recast as an Absa-arranged facility guaranteed by US EXIM, which remains unsigned. No Afreximbank, Standard Bank, BFA, Sonangol, Endiama or IDC involvement was found.

### Cited Findings
**FSDEA / ASF Yova**
- The US$15m bridging loan was entered into on 7 Aug 2023 between Pensana and ASF Yova Mining Holding Limited, "FSDEA's wholly owned subsidiary" — [RNS 15 May 2025](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/drawdown-of-first-usd-25-million-of-mine-fina-/8879823)
