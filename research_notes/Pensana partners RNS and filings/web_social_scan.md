## 5. Controversies: legal, community, environmental, regulator, short-seller

### Takeaway
I found no legal disputes, regulator actions or environmental incidents. The live controversy concerns the credibility of the Qatar/Cascade financing: the US$150m is outstanding, and the sheikh's board appointment was announced and then clarified as not yet effective. ShareProphets is pushing this hard (an activist bear blog, not a formal short report). The schedule has slipped to late 2028 and Coola has been farmed out.

### Cited Findings
- **Fact:** Construction is over 30% complete. Design, high-cost site work and long-lead procurement have been "ramped down until the financing has been finalised", and the schedule is extended "from the previously announced 2028 to late 2028". Pensana is in "detailed financing discussions" with major shareholders and has negotiated with potential offtakers to support ABSA and EXIM debt. — [RNS 25 Sep 2026](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Update-on-Longonjo-Financing-25th-September-2026.pdf)
- **Fact:** Coola has been farmed out to ASX-listed Tyranna (TYX). Tyranna will spend US$2.6m over 3 years, diluting Pensana to 20%, with an option for Coola ore to be treated at Longonjo. — [RNS 25 Sep 2026](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Update-on-Longonjo-Financing-25th-September-2026.pdf)
- **Fact:** The sheikh's appointment was not effective and not filed in Guernsey as of 17 Aug 2026, two months after it was announced (18 Jun). — [RNS 17 Aug 2026](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Update-on-Strategic-Investor-Board-Appointment.pdf); [LSE news 18 Jun](https://www.lse.co.uk/news/in-brief-pensana-investor-cascade-adds-supportive-sheikh-to-board-5vfa2jkxkt1ki65.html)
- **Opinion/allegation:** ShareProphets tags its pieces "fake sheikh", calls something "a lie", and says Longonjo is "effectively shuttered". These are unverified, and the article bodies are paywalled. — [ShareProphets 88441](https://shareprophets.com/views/88441/vindication-pensana-the-150m-cheque-from-qatar-is-still-not-in-the-post-any-update-on-the-fake-or-not-fake-sheikh); [ShareProphets 88481](https://shareprophets.com/views/88481/pensana-two-questions-paul-atherley-must-answer)

Ozango says it holds an approved environmental licence and a life-of-mine ESIA, both published on its site. — [Ver Angola](https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/092026/MateriasPrimas/50045/Longonjo-volta-a-surpreender-descoberta-de-novas-terras-raras-eleva-potencial-da-mina.htm); [ozango.co.ao](https://www.ozango.co.ao/)
