- Listed under "Pending Environmental Category A Projects": **"Pensana Longonjo Rare Earths Mine"**, Angola. The location is given as "60 kilometers west of Huambo and 30 kilometers north of Benguela".
  - EXIM reference AP778661XX
  - Posted 02/18/2026
  - Estimated annual CO2: TBD
  - ESIA: "Not yet publicly available"
- The page does not name an obligor/borrower, a US exporter or an amount. It has no entries for Ozango, Absa or Sable.
- **What this confirms:** an application for Longonjo is formally in EXIM's pipeline. This is EXIM's own record, not Pensana's claim.
- **What it does not confirm:** it is not an approval. EXIM's procedures require a Category A project's ESIA to be public for at least 30 days before final approval. Because the ESIA is not yet posted, final EXIM approval cannot come sooner than about 30 days after it appears.
  - Inference: once the ESIA is posted, it gives a visible lead indicator of an approval.
  - Transactions over US$100m also need a notice to Congress, which is a second visible signal. Check this.
- **Timing:** the listing was posted 18 Feb 2026, before Cascade was named (4 Mar 2026). It has gone eight months without an ESIA being posted.
- For comparison, NioCorp's Elk Creek (USA) has sat in the same Category A list since 11/08/2023, also without an ESIA posted. Long dwell times on this list are not unusual.
