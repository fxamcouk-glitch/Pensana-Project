## 1. EXIM (exim.gov): board items, press releases, SCRI, Congress and Federal Register notices

### Takeaway
On EXIM's side, the only thing that exists is a listing of the Longonjo mine on EXIM's **pending** Environmental Category A transactions page, posted 18 Feb 2026, with the ESIA not yet public. That shows an application is in the pipeline at EXIM. There is no board approval, no Federal Register notice of a >US$100m final-commitment application, no Sunshine Act agenda item and no EXIM press release naming Pensana, Angola rare earths or Absa. The "Letter of Interest for up to 100% of US$160m" is sourced only to Pensana.

### Cited Findings
- **Agency says:** EXIM's "Pending Transactions for Environmental Category A and B" page lists "Pensana Longonjo Rare Earths Mine | 60 kilometers west of Huambo and 30 kilometers north of Benguela, Angola | AP778661XX | CO2: TBD | Posting date 02/18/2026 | ESIA: Not yet publicly available". In the same table are Kozloduy (posted 05/28/2026) and NioCorp Elk Creek (MMIA, posted 11/08/2023) — [EXIM pending transactions](https://www.exim.gov/policies/exim-bank-and-environment/pending-transactions)
- Category A listing is the environmental-disclosure step before a board decision. The page says Category A projects need an ESIA or comparable document — [EXIM pending transactions](https://www.exim.gov/policies/exim-bank-and-environment/pending-transactions)
- **Federal Register, Section 2(b)(3)-type notices:** EXIM published 12 "Application for Final Commitment for a Long-Term Loan or Financial Guarantee in Excess of $100 Million" notices between 28 Nov 2025 and 14 Oct 2026. All were read in full on GovInfo, and **none concerns Pensana, Angola, rare earths or Absa**. The obligors were Silk Way West Airlines (AP300185XX, 14 Oct 2026), SPV/Astranis (AP300125XX), Tele-Fonika Cable Americas (AP300003XX), Mesabi Metallics (AP300126XX, 17 Aug 2026), Firefly Aerospace (AP300129XX), Korean Air (AP300072XX), Jeju Air (AP300067XX), Gunes Ekspres (AP300026XX), Perpetua Resources Idaho (AP768324XX, 30 Mar 2026), Ethiopian Airlines (AP300102XX), a "Legally Independent Delaware Entity" (AP300117XX) and CesiumAstro (AP774431XX) — [Federal Register EXIM notices list via API](https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents.json?conditions[agencies][]=export-import-bank&conditions[publication_date][gte]=2025-09-01); e.g. [14 Oct 2026 notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/14/2026-20933/application-for-final-commitment-for-a-long-term-loan-or-financial-guarantee-in-excess-of-100), [17 Aug 2026 Mesabi](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/17/2026-16688/application-for-final-commitment-for-a-long-term-loan-or-financial-guarantee-in-excess-of-100), [30 Mar 2026 Perpetua](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/30/2026-06117/application-for-final-commitment-for-a-long-term-loan-or-financial-guarantee-in-excess-of-100)
- A Federal Register full-text search for "Pensana", "Longonjo" and "Ozango" (all agencies, since June 2024) returned **0 documents** — [Federal Register API, term=Pensana](https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents.json?conditions[term]=Pensana)
- The only EXIM Sunshine Act notice in the period (22 Apr 2026) covered just "U.S. Content Change for the Working Capital Guarantee Program" — [Federal Register 2026-07848](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/22/2026-07848/sunshine-act-meetings)
- **EXIM press release, 7 Aug 2026 (White House roundtable):** "$58M in three major new rare earths deals". The companies, per the White House fact sheet, are 5E Advanced Materials (US$8m), Westwater Resources (US$25m) and Global Advanced Materials (US$25m). Pensana is not among them — [EXIM release](https://www.exim.gov/news/trump-administration-announces-58m-major-new-critical-mineral-deals-white-house-roundtable); [White House fact sheet](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-billions-in-new-deals-and-investments-to-power-american-mining/)
- An EXIM newsroom search for "Angola" surfaced no Pensana or Angola rare-earth release. The hits were a Chairman Jovanovic speech (17 Jul 2026), an Argentina framework, a Pennsylvania visit and the 7 Aug roundtable release, and none of these mention Pensana or Longonjo — [EXIM news search](https://www.exim.gov/news?search_api_fulltext=Angola); [Jovanovic 17 Jul 2026](https://www.exim.gov/news/chairman-jovanovic-outlines-exims-role-securing-supply-chains-and-rebuilding-industrial-base)
- **SCRI rules (agency says):** "EXIM will provide financing for international projects with **signed long-term 'off-take' contracts with U.S. companies**… the financed amount depends on the amount of the off-take contract between the foreign project and the U.S. importer… The financing amount will be determined by the percentage of the mine's off-take that is with the United States… the project cannot be controlled by the PRC." — [EXIM SCRI page](https://exim.gov/about/special-initiatives/supply-chain-resiliency-initiative)
- **Pensana says (not EXIM):** on 28 Oct 2025 Pensana announced "advanced engagement" with EXIM, "supported by a Letter of Interest ('LOI') to participate up to 100% in the US$160 million debt funding", qualifying via the VAC/eVAC offtake MOU under SCRI — [Pensana RNS 28 Oct 2025](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/u-s-exim-debt-funding-to-accelerate-u-s-min-/9197987)

### Inferences
- EXIM has a live application file (AP778661XX) dating from at least Feb 2026, so the relationship goes beyond an LOI. It is still pre-approval, because the ESIA has not been posted and Category A disclosure normally runs for a period before a board vote.
- A US$160m guarantee would be over US$100m, so before final commitment it would normally produce a Federal Register "Application for Final Commitment" notice and a congressional notification. Up to 14 Oct 2026, no such notice exists. EXIM therefore had not reached the final-commitment stage on Longonjo.
- SCRI requires *signed* offtake with US companies, and the size of the financing scales with the US share. Pensana's offtakes are non-binding MOUs, and the eVAC one runs through VAC's German parent (see the partners report). "Up to 100%" of US$160m therefore depends on US-bound binding offtake that does not yet exist. The EXIM LOI itself is a low-threshold, non-binding product that is available online ("EXIM Online Letter of Interest" appears in the site navigation).

### Gaps
- EXIM does not publish LOIs, so EXIM could not independently confirm the LOI's existence, date or amount. The pending-transactions listing indirectly corroborates EXIM engagement.
- No EXIM board agenda or minutes naming the deal were found. EXIM's Section 2(b)(3) reports to Congress are not posted in a searchable form, so they could not be checked directly. The Federal Register notices are the public proxy for them.
- EXIM's China and Transformational Exports Program pages were not found to mention Pensana. Only the navigation was reviewed, not a full-text check.

## 2. DFC (dfc.gov): projects, grants, Lobito Corridor

### Takeaway
DFC is the only US agency with **disbursed money** to Pensana. On 4 Dec 2024, during Biden's Angola visit, it committed a **US$3.4m technical-assistance (feasibility) grant**. USAspending shows US$1.95m outlaid and a period of performance that **ended 24 Sep 2026**. In 2026 DFC also profiled the project as "Project Development funding" for refining facilities. No DFC loan, equity, guarantee or political risk insurance for Pensana or Ozango was found. The DFC support for eVAC is separate.

### Cited Findings
- **Agency says (4 Dec 2024):** among Lobito Corridor transactions announced as DFC CEO Scott Nathan accompanied President Biden to Angola: "The commitment of a $3.4 million technical assistance grant to Pensana to conduct feasibility studies to advance development of a rare earth mine and refining facility in Longonjo, Angola, along the Lobito Corridor." The same release covered the US$553m Lobito Atlantic Railway loan — [DFC press release](https://www.dfc.gov/media/press-releases/dfc-announces-investments-supporting-development-along-lobito-corridor)
- **USAspending (DFC data):** award ID 9000116961; recipient "PENSANA RARE EARTH TA" (UEI QPQ7CJQWRCV4, located in the UK); description "FEASIBILITY STUDY FOR MID-STREAM PROCESSING OF RARE EARTH MINERALS IN ANGOLA"; obligation US$3,400,000; **total outlay US$1,954,085**; signed 24 Sep 2024; period of performance 24 Sep 2024 to **24 Sep 2026**; place of performance Angola; office "Office of Acquisition". Only one transaction is recorded, the original award, with no modifications — [USAspending award](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/ASST_NON_9000116961_077); [API](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/awards/ASST_NON_9000116961_077/)
- This is the **only federal award of any type** (grants, loans, contracts, direct payments, other) to a recipient matching "Pensana" in USAspending for FY2008 to FY2026. A recipient search for "Ozango" returned only unrelated names (Kozango, Sozango LLC), and "Longonjo" returned nothing — [USAspending API](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/search/spending_by_award/)
- **Agency says (2026 investment story):** "DFC committed Project Development funding to help Pensana Plc… advance development of mineral refining facilities in Angola… Pensana will use the DFC project development support to conduct testing at Angola's Longonjo… mine". It cites Stage 1 output of 20ktpa MREC in 2027 and 40ktpa in 2029, the VAC/eVAC offtake, and proximity to the DFC-backed Lobito Railway. The page footer says "This project was profiled in 2026." — [DFC investment story](https://www.dfc.gov/investment-story/expanding-rare-earth-processing-angola-production-us)
- **Pensana says:** the DFC grant was US$3.4m, of which about US$0.5m had been drawn by June 2025 (per the FY25 annual report, cited in the partners report) — [AR25](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Pensana-integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf)
- DFC's 7 Aug 2026 roundtable deliverable in Africa was a "matching $4.8 million investment into **Harena Rare Earths**… in Madagascar". There was no Pensana item — [White House fact sheet](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-billions-in-new-deals-and-investments-to-power-american-mining/)

### Inferences
- The "Project Development funding" in the 2026 story appears to be the same US$3.4m technical-assistance grant described in DFC's newer terminology. No separate award appears in USAspending (data through FY2026). The 2026 profile restates Pensana's 2027 production date, which Pensana itself has since moved to late 2028.
- The grant's period of performance ended on 24 Sep 2026 with about 57% outlaid in USAspending. Either it was extended without a modification being reported yet, or the remainder will lapse. This is worth checking with Pensana's FY2026 report.

### Gaps
- DFC's Active Projects database page is JavaScript-rendered, and a curl search for "Pensana" returned no server-side matches. A manual browser check is advisable — [DFC active projects](https://www.dfc.gov/what-we-do/active-projects)
- No DFC board minutes or Public Information Summary for Pensana were found. Technical-assistance grants usually do not get one.

## 4. White House: the "10 Aug 2026" briefing

### Takeaway
The event Pensana's CEO attended was President Trump's **"Roundtable on American Mining" at the State Department (Ben Franklin Room) on Friday 7 August 2026**. Pensana's RNS about it is dated Monday 10 Aug, so the brief's "10 Aug" date refers to the RNS, not the event. The White House fact sheet lists about US$2bn of named awards plus US$180m for mining schools (press called it "US$3bn"). **Pensana, Angola and Longonjo are absent** from the fact sheet, the EXIM release and Rubio's remarks. A whitehouse.gov site search for "Pensana" returns "No results found". No attendee list was published.

### Cited Findings
- **Agency says (White House, 7 Aug 2026):** "President Donald J. Trump held a historic roundtable with the mining industry where he announced over $2 billion in critical mining and mining-related projects… and over $180 million in mining school investments." The named items:
  - Department of War: Strategic Bauxite (>US$85m), Niron Magnetics (US$150m), Sila Nanotechnologies (US$1.4bn), Sunrise Energy Metals (US$400m)
  - EXIM: 5E Advanced Materials (US$8m), Westwater (US$25m), Global Advanced Materials (US$25m)
  - DFC: matching US$4.8m into Harena Rare Earths (Madagascar)
  - DOE: US$100m to 14 mining schools; DoW: >US$80m to three schools
  - "the largest meeting of industry leaders and the President in over 120 years"
  — [White House fact sheet](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-billions-in-new-deals-and-investments-to-power-american-mining/)
- Press reports put attendance at 200+ mining executives, investors and officials and called the package US$3bn — [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2622904/us-govt-to-invest-3b-in-minerals); [IDN Financials](https://www.idnfinancials.com/news/67178/trump-sets-aside-us3bn-for-u-s-strategic-minerals-projects)
- whitehouse.gov search for "Pensana": "No results found." — [WH search](https://www.whitehouse.gov/?s=Pensana)
- **Pensana says (RNS 10 Aug 2026):** "Pensana CEO Tim George was one of the few international mining executives invited to a private briefing with the President… Secretary of State Marco Rubio and other senior State Department officials". After the briefing, George "had the opportunity to discuss the Company's role… with David Copley of the Executive Office of the President and National Security Council". The RNS announces no US funding for Pensana. It restates Longonjo as a "US$250 million development… approximately 25% complete", with first MREC in 2028 — [Pensana RNS 10 Aug 2026](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/prn/pensana--pre/trump-announces-us-3-billion-support-for-u-s-/9712159)

### Inferences
- With 200+ attendees, being "invited" was attendance at a large public-facing event (the WH calls it a "roundtable", not a private briefing). Pensana's RNS headline ("Trump announces US$3 billion support…") borrows the government's headline, but none of that money was for Pensana.
- No Africa critical-minerals executive order naming Angola or Pensana was found.

### Gaps
- No official attendee list exists publicly, so George's attendance rests only on Pensana's word. The government-side status of the Copley conversation is unknown.
