# Sheikh Dr Badr bin Dalhim Al-Faheid Al-Hajri: public professional profile

Scope: public, professional information only, as at 19 Oct 2026. Home addresses and family details are left out on purpose. One search result is a TikTok page and one is a news story about his house. Those were used only for the nationality statement in the news story; location details are left out.

Evidence labels: **CONFIRMED** = a primary or first-hand source (RNS, a named newspaper reporting the person directly). **REPORTED** = secondary or aggregator. **UNVERIFIED** = self-description or inference.

## Q1. Exact name and variants

### Takeaway
The same individual appears under several transliterations. Arabic-language Qatari press consistently uses **بدر بن دلهم الفهيد الهاجري**, and one source adds the grandfather's name, **بدر بن دلهم بن علي الفهيد الهاجري**. He uses "Dr" (د.) everywhere. "Sheikh" appears only in Pensana's RNS and the English financial press that copied it. Qatari Arabic press uses "Dr" and once "His Excellency" (سعادة).

### Inferences

## Q3. Business and other roles

### Takeaway
His only documented organisational role, apart from the proposed Cascade seat, is with the **Arab Union for Artificial Intelligence and Programming** (الاتحاد العربي للذكاء الاصطناعي والبرمجة). This is a recently founded "specialised Arab union" under the Council of Arab Economic Unity (CAEU), a body of the Arab League. He was deputy president (Feb 2026), then president (Mar 2026). Press otherwise describes him generically as a "businessman and investor". No operating company, Qatar Financial Centre (QFC) entity or listed-company directorship was found.
