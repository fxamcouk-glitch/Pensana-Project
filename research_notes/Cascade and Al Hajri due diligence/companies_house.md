- **AR25 on security:** the external facility (12%, lender warrants, "UK-based investment house") is secured by charges over Pensana's shares in Sable Min Unipessoal Lda, SBLRTHS Unipessoal Lda and Saltend Magnet Metals Limited (earlier notes, citing [AR25 p.119](https://pensana.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Pensana-integrated-annual-report-2025.pdf)).

### Inferences
- The debenture's Charged Shares definition covers the subsidiary shares that AR25 says secure the 12% facility. Its March 2025 facility date also matches. Together these make it highly likely that the **"UK-based investment house" 12% facility is the RiverFort facility.** The accounts still do not name the lender.
- Satisfaction of the charge on 5 Mar 2026 implies the RiverFort facility was repaid or released by then. If so, that 12% borrowing (about $2.6m at 31 Dec 2025) should be absent or reduced at 30 Jun 2026. This is unconfirmed until the FY2026 accounts are out.
- **The FSDEA/ASF Yova bridge is said to be secured on Pensana's Ozango shares.** That security would be taken under Angolan or Portuguese law over a non-UK subsidiary's shares. Only charges created by a UK company over its own property are registrable at CH, so its absence from CH is not evidence that it does not exist. The same applies to any Cascade security at Sable (Portugal) level or Absa project security at Ozango (Angola) level.
- **There is no registered security in favour of Cascade or Absa at UK level as of 19 Oct 2026.** This is consistent with the Absa debt not having closed. Note that an MR01 can be filed up to 21 days after creation, so a charge created in late Sep or Oct 2026 might not yet appear.

- I did not read the full 35-page debenture: pages 6–35 are image-only and I read pp. 1–5. The facility amount, interest and maturity are not on the CH form.
- No public record identifies what replaced the RiverFort facility, if anything.
