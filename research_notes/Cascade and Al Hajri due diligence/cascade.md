## 4. QKR Corp / QKR Namibia / Navachab: history and aftermath

### Takeaway
QKR was founded in 2012 by Pengilly and Jan Kulczyk. Its largest backers were **Qatar Holding (part of QIA)** and Kulczyk Investments, which together provided most of its funds. QKR bought Navachab from AngloGold Ashanti for an enterprise value of US$110m, completing on 30 Jun 2014, and sold 7.5% to Namibia's Epangelo. In 2015 it stopped new investment after Qatar reviewed its mining allocation and Kulczyk died. **No evidence was found of a 2019 sale of Navachab to Auryn Mining.** Namibian press shows QKR still majority owner, exploring a sale through UBS in Oct 2021, and still owner when it raised US$155m of expansion debt in Jun 2024.

### Cited Findings
- On 10 Feb 2014, AngloGold Ashanti agreed to sell its Navachab subsidiary to a wholly owned subsidiary of QKR Corporation Limited. The price was an enterprise value of US$110m plus a deferred net smelter return of 2% of ounces sold, subject to a gold-price threshold and a cap — [AngloGold 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067428/000120561314000025/aga_announcement.htm); [Mining Weekly 10 Feb 2014](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/private-company-buys-navachab-from-anglogold-2014-02-10)
- The deal completed on 30 Jun 2014, with Epangelo partnering QKR — [Mining Weekly 11 Jul 2014](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/private-miner-completes-acquisition-of-anglogolds-navachab-2014-07-11); [Miningmx](https://www.miningmx.com/off-the-wires/22549-anglogold-completes-navachab-sale/)
- QKR sold 7.5% of Navachab to Epangelo Mining — [Miningmx](https://www.miningmx.com/off-the-wires/11608-qkr-sells-7-5-in-navachab-to-epangelo/)
- **Backers:** Reuters, via Miningmx (2015), reports that QKR's largest backers were Qatar Holding, part of QIA, and Kulczyk Investments. Each "provided almost half of QKR's funds". Qatar's wealth fund was reviewing its mining strategy, and a mining executive said "the Qataris wanted to reduce their investment in QKR" — [Miningmx "Qatari second thoughts negative for QKR"](https://www.miningmx.com/off-the-wires/13150-qatari-second-thoughts-negative-for-qkr/); [TradeArabia](https://www.tradearabia.com/news/IND_283867.html)
- Bloomberg (Jul 2015): QKR scrapped its expansion and would make no new investments, and the acquisition team was disbanded. It had struggled to win backing from its Qatari backer after management changes at the sovereign fund, and co-investor Jan Kulczyk had died. A roughly US$1bn approach for Nevsun had stalled. QIA declined to comment — [BizNews/Bloomberg](https://www.biznews.com/briefs/2015/07/30/former-jse-star-lloyd-pengillys-qkr-scraps-expansion-plans-on-mining-rout)
- On 15 Oct 2021, QKR Namibia Mineral Holdings (92.5%, with Epangelo 7.5%) began a "comprehensive review of strategic alternatives", including a possible sale, with UBS as adviser. The article says QKR was "set up by Lloyd Pengilly and Jan Kulczyk". Spokesperson Usi Hoëbeb is named — [The Namibian, 15 Oct 2021](https://www.namibian.com.na/qkr-explores-exit-from-navachab)
- On 6 Jun 2024, RMB arranged a US$155m (about N$2.9bn) syndicated loan for Navachab's expansion. Owners are QKR Namibia Mineral Holdings and "JG Investment Investments" (together 92.5%, sic) and Epangelo (7.5%). QKR Namibia's MD is George Botshiwe. Pengilly is not mentioned — [Namibian Sun, 6 Jun 2024](https://namibiansun.com/mw-main/qkr-secures-n2.9-billion-for-navachab-expansion2024-06-06118681); [Windhoek Express/we.com.na Aug 2024](https://www.we.com.na/focus-mining/navachab-production-shines-expansion-funded2024-08-06121372)
- A search for a 2019 Navachab sale to Auryn Mining returned nothing — [search results incl. The Namibian](https://namibian.com.na/?p=640153)

### Inferences
- The premise of a "2019 sale to Auryn Mining" is **not supported** by the sources found. Navachab remained under QKR Namibia in 2021 and 2024. Any later ownership change was not found.
- The QKR precedent is relevant to Cascade: a Pengilly vehicle backed by a Qatari sovereign entity made one acquisition and then stalled when the Qatari side reviewed its commitment. Cascade's US$150m tranche is similarly contingent on Qatari backers. This is an analogy, not evidence about Cascade.
- Pengilly's role at QKR after 2015 is unclear. The UK holding company is now Kulczyk-controlled, and 2024 press does not name him.

### Gaps
